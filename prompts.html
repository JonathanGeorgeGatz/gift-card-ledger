THE GIFT CARD LEDGER — the three prompts, exactly as typed on camera
=====================================================================

PROMPT 1 (the innocent one — produces wrong build v1, shipped as v1_engine.js)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Here is the export from my gift card program (giftcards.csv:
date,card_id,event,amount — the events are issue, reload, redeem). Build me a
single-file web page I can paste it into. Tell me how much money is still
sitting on open cards, and which cards have gone quiet.

PROMPT 2 (the frame — produces wrong build v2, shipped as v2_engine.js)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rebuild it under these rules, exactly:
- Money is integer cents end to end; never floats. Round half up wherever
  division happens, and round each displayed figure ONCE, from cents.
- The amount is always positive; the EVENT carries the direction. A signed
  amount is refused, never interpreted.
- Replay each card's events in date order, ties broken by original row order.
- A card whose history is impossible is FLAGGED by id and excluded from every
  total: a card whose first event is a redemption, and a card that at any
  point gives out more than it holds. Flags are read aloud.
- The page owns no clock. "Now" is the latest date in the file, and a card's
  age is measured from its LAST visit, not its first sale.
- Dormant = an open card quiet for the dormancy window (months of 30 days).
- The holiday wave: over cards first sold in a November or December, how much
  of that value came back within the window dial's days OF THAT CARD'S SALE.
- Any row the page cannot read is REFUSED out loud with its row number and
  reason. Never guess.
- Dials, all adjustable: dormancy months, wave window days, crumb line.

PROMPT 3 (the lawyer pass — produces the shipped page)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Harden it like it will be audited:
- Derive the calendar from first principles (leap years included); an
  impossible date is refused by name, never rolled forward.
- Before rendering anything, cross-examine yourself: assemble the liability
  two independent ways and reconcile to the cent; re-add the aging buckets
  against the headline; re-derive every card's balance from its event totals;
  re-add the dormant and crumb figures and bound them by the liability; cap
  refused rows and flagged cards at a tenth each.
- If any audit fails, or either share passes 10%, render nothing but one
  line: DO NOT BOOK THIS NUMBER.
- No network calls, no clock: "today" is whatever the file says.
- Keep the dials labeled as judgments, and show the refusals, the flags and
  the audits on the page where the user can read them.
