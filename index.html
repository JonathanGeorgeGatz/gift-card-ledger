The Gift Card Ledger

Paste the one file your gift card program already exports — every issue, reload and redemption — and it tells you what you still owe: the outstanding balance, how old each open card is, which ones have gone quiet, and how much of your holiday card money actually comes back. Everything is computed from the file; the page has no clock and sends nothing anywhere.
